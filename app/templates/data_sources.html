Data Sources for Our AI Model

At UrFit.io, we value trust and transparency. To provide accurate and reliable insights, our AI model relies on publicly available data from reputable sources in the baseball world. Below is a list of the primary data sources that may be considered in our training process:

  • Baseball Reference (baseball-reference.com)
  • Baseball Almanac (baseball-almanac.com)
  • Perfect Game (perfectgame.org)
  • Prep Baseball Report (prepbaseballreport.com)
  • NCAA Baseball (ncaa.com)
  • Other Websites

    We also incorporate data from other trusted baseball platforms, social media and news sources to ensure a diverse and comprehensive dataset.

If you have any questions about our data sources or AI model, feel free to email us at support@urfit.io.
