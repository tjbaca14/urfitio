Our Mission

In the world of amateur sports, players and parents often invest significant time and effort into marketing themselves to coaches and scouts. The narrative pushed by the industry suggests that athletes "need" their services—when, in truth, the reality is quite the opposite: they need you.

At UrFit.io, we combine our experience as former amateur and professional athletes with cutting-edge technology to empower young athletes. Our goal is to provide the knowledge and insights necessary for making informed career decisions, helping them take their game to the next level with confidence and clarity.

Meet Our Team

Tucker Baca, Co-Founder & Founding Engineer

Tucker is the founding engineer of UrFit.io, a platform born from his passion for technology, sports, and a desire to empower young athletes to make informed career decisions. A former collegiate baseball player at Arizona State University and St. Katherine's, Tucker also played professionally for the Baltimore Orioles in the MiLB. His tumultuous baseball career, marked by uninformed decisions and missed opportunities, became the driving force behind creating UrFit.io.

With a degree in Mathematics from ASU and extensive experience as a Machine Learning/AI engineer and architect, Tucker leverages cutting-edge technology to solve complex problems. UrFit.io bridges the worlds of athletics and innovation, providing athletes with the insights and tools needed to make smarter decisions and achieve their goals. For Tucker, UrFit.io is more than a platform—it's a personal mission to help the next generation of athletes avoid the challenges he once faced.

Kyler Berrett, Co-Founder

Kyler is a lifelong baseball enthusiast and former Georgia state champion who excelled as an undersized shortstop in high school. Despite a standout career, he experienced the challenges of finding the right collegiate fit, sparking his passion for creating UrFit.io. Drawing from his personal journey, Kyler is dedicated to empowering athletes with the tools and insights needed to make informed, data-driven decisions that align with their athletic and academic goals. Outside of baseball, Kyler thrives in business, holding a degree in Economics from BYU and an MBA from SMU.
